Sharpening machines and tools

Alemão® was born in Votuporanga, in the state of São Paulo, Brazil, with one focus: machines, tools and parts to sharpen cuticle nippers, scissors and knives. Today there are more than 500 machines sold, more than 300 active customers, deliveries all over Brazil and machines already exported to the United States, Portugal and Italy.

/// FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Didn't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp.

Ask a question
How do I order?

Choose the machine or part and tap “Request a quote” or “Order”. WhatsApp opens with the message ready. The team confirms availability, shipping and payment method.

What are your business hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 am to 8 pm (Brasília time), on WhatsApp +55 (17) 99706-9595. Messages sent outside business hours are answered on the next business day.

Do you ship to my country?

We ship all over Brazil and have already exported machines to the United States, Portugal and Italy. Shipping cost and delivery time are quoted on WhatsApp, according to your location.

What's the difference between the A1 and the A2?

The A2 has a speed controller. The A1 costs R$ 4,350.00 and measures 30 × 33 × 46 (H × W × L). The A2 costs R$ 5,850.00 and measures 38 × 33 × 46 (H × W × L).

Do the machines come in different colors?

Yes: all Alemão machines are available in orange and green. Choose the color in the quote form (or with the color button at the top of the site).

Do you sell tools and spare parts?

Yes: 9 tools (guillotine, tip aligner, joint play remover, knife jig, hammer, wheel dressers and motor) and 14 spare parts (ramps, flanges, pulleys, shafts, kits and more). See tools and parts.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. All machines come with a 90-day warranty, counted from delivery. Tools and parts also have the 90-day legal warranty under Brazil's Consumer Protection Code. See the Terms and conditions of sale (in Portuguese).

Is there a video of the machine working?

Yes, see the Videos section or the page with all videos.
